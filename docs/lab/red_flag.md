## What this means

- Race control's reds in this data follow either a late neutralisation (a restart beats finishing behind the Safety Car) or damage car data cannot see (barrier, gravel, debris, rain). Only the first is visible here, so our red comes only late in the race: our VSC or SC out for a live incident with 2 to 4 laps left, from a race length computed from the lap length before the race.
- The old red by time at a crash site caught two mid-race reds (2023 Australia, Albon; 2023 Mexico City, Magnussen) but sent many more reds race control never called. A long recovery now only adds an advisory to the crash sector's flag.
- Five reds in 20 races is very little data. Treat these rules as a transparent starting point, not a measured red flag accuracy: race control also has CCTV, marshal reports and medical information.
